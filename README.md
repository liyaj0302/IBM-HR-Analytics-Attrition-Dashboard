# 📊 IBM HR Analytics: Workforce Retention & Attrition Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Executive HR Analytics Dashboard built in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze employee turnover, identify high-risk attrition cohorts, and propose data-backed talent retention strategies for a workforce of **1,470 employees**.

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Headcount:** 1,470 Active Employee Records
- **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.1% (237 Total Exits)
- **Average Monthly Salary:** $7,241.00
- **Average Service Tenure:** 7.0 Years

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Excel Techniques Used
- **Advanced Excel Formulas:** `COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `ROUND`, `IF`
- **Data Preprocessing:** Zero-variance feature pruning, text-to-numeric indicator mapping, categorical binning for Age Groups & Tenure.
- **Dynamic Data Modeling:** 6 Pivot Charts integrated into a symmetric 2x3 grid layout.
- **User Interactivity:** Multi-select cross-filtering slicers (Department, Education Field, Age Group, Marital Status).

---

## 📈 Strategic Business Insights
1. **Tenure Vulnerability:** Peak turnover occurs within the first 0–2 years of employment (**29.8% Attrition Rate**).
2. **Marital Risk Factor:** Single employees experience **25.5% Attrition**, more than double that of married or divorced staff.
3. **Commute Impact:** Distance strain plays a major role, with far commutes (15+ km) showing **20.7% Attrition**.
4. **Financial ROI:** Reducing overall attrition by 5% saves ~73 employees, yielding **~$1.2M** in recruitment & onboarding cost avoidance.
